# How Water Bill is Calculated

Your water bill is calculated based on a cumulative tiered pricing model (Bậc thang). The more water you use, the higher the price per cubic meter (m³) for the additional usage.

## Current Rate Tiers (VND/m³):

1.  **Tier 1** (0 - 10 m³): **5,973 VND**
2.  **Tier 2** (10 - 20 m³): **7,052 VND**
3.  **Tier 3** (20 - 30 m³): **8,669 VND**
4.  **Tier 4** (Over 30 m³): **15,929 VND**

## Calculation Example:
If you use **35 m³** in a month:
*   First 10 m³: 10 * 5,973 = 59,730 VND
*   Next 10 m³: 10 * 7,052 = 70,520 VND
*   Next 10 m³: 10 * 8,669 = 86,690 VND
*   Remaining 5 m³: 5 * 15,929 = 79,645 VND
*   **Total Bill**: 296,585 VND (before tax/fees)

*The system automatically calculates this daily and sums it up for the monthly total.*
