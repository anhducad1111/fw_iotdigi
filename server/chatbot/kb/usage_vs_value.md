# Meter Value vs. Consumption

It is important to distinguish between "Value" and "Consumption" when reading your dashboard.

## 1. Value (Raw Reading)
*   **Definition**: The number you see on the physical water meter or the "Total Volume" on the dashboard.
*   **Behavior**: This number **always increases** and never resets (unless the meter is replaced/reset).
*   **Unit**: Cubic meters (m³).

## 2. Consumption (Usage)
*   **Definition**: The amount of water used during a specific period (Hour, Day, Month).
*   **Calculation**: `End Value` - `Start Value` of the period.
*   **Example**:
    *   Meter reading at 8:00 AM: 100.0 m³
    *   Meter reading at 9:00 AM: 100.5 m³
    *   **Consumption**: 0.5 m³

*The chatbot usually discusses "Consumption" unless you specifically ask for the current meter reading.*
