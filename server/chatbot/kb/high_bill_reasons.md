# Why is my bill high?

If your water bill is higher than expected, consider the following common reasons:

1.  **Hidden Leaks**:
    *   Leaking toilets ("running" toilets) are the #1 cause of high water bills. They can waste up to 200 gallons a day.
    *   Dripping faucets.

2.  **Tiered Pricing Impact**:
    *   Because of the tiered pricing structure (Bậc thang), pushing your usage into **Tier 4 (>30m³)** makes the additional water almost **3x more expensive** than Tier 1.
    *   Example: Saving just 5m³ to stay under Tier 4 can significantly reduce costs.

3.  **Seasonal Usage**:
    *   Watering gardens or filling pools in summer.
    *   Increase in house guests.

4.  **Meter Reading Error**:
    *   Although rare with digital sensors, verify the "Current Value" on the dashboard matches your physical meter.
